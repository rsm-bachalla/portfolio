About

Not one thing.

I've spent my career at the intersection of product, data, and technology — mostly at startups where the job description doesn't really exist yet. I've shipped features, built data pipelines, run growth experiments, and worked closely with customers to figure out what actually matters.

I'm drawn to problems where the answer isn't obvious — where you need to bring together user intuition, analytical rigor, and a willingness to build and break things. I like working across functions: with engineers, designers, sales teams, and sometimes directly with the data itself.

Right now, I'm particularly excited about AI — not as a buzzword, but as a genuine tool for automating the tedious, augmenting the analytical, and unlocking things that weren't possible before.
